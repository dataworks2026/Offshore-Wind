### [w8-logistics:weather_windows]
Offshore repair scheduling depends on weather windows with significant wave height below 1.5 m and wind speeds under 12 m/s. Average waiting time in winter exceeds 18 days, extending risk exposure.

### [w8-logistics:vessel_planning]
Crew transfer vessels require 48-hour mobilization lead time. Coordinating blade repairs with nacelle component swaps optimizes deck usage and reduces repeated mobilizations.

### [w8-logistics:contingency]
Maintain contingency plans for rapid shutdown if weather deteriorates mid-repair. Document fallback procedures to minimize partially completed repairs that could worsen damage.
