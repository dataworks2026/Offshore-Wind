### [w1-repair:surface_prep]
ISO 61400-23 requires technicians abrade damaged laminate to a taper ratio of 1:50 before applying composite plies, ensuring adhesive compatibility and avoiding void nucleation. Surface dryness must be below 12% relative humidity to achieve specified bond strength.

### [w1-repair:cure_validation]
Post-cure validation involves differential scanning calorimetry confirming resin degree of cure above 0.9 and infrared thermography verifying uniform temperature within ±5 °C during curing blankets. Deviations mandate destructive coupon pulls before the blade returns to service.

### [w1-repair:post_repair_monitoring]
Operators schedule follow-up ultrasonic inspections 30 days after repair to confirm no sub-surface porosity or crack propagation. ISO guidance suggests trending shear wave amplitude reductions to ensure residual defects remain under acceptance thresholds.
