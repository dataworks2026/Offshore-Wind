### [w1-erosion:baseline]
Leading-edge erosion initiates when rain, hail, or particulates impact the protective coating at tip speeds exceeding 80 m/s. Early-stage wear appears as matte streaking and minor pitting concentrated between 20–40% span, often without exposed fibers.

### [w1-erosion:progression]
Once coating is breached, composite plies experience micro-cracking that increases drag and acoustic emissions. Field observations show that unchecked erosion can progress one severity grade within 3–6 months under high precipitation regimes.

### [w1-erosion:mitigation]
Applying polyurethane leading-edge protection during shoulder seasons limits additional damage, but technicians must prepare the surface to avoid adhesion failures. Interim drone inspections at 45-day intervals help confirm stability.
