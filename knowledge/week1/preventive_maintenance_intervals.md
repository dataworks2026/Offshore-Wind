### [w1-intervals:baseline]
DNV standard ST-0262 recommends offshore rotor blades receive tactile leading-edge assessments every 6 months when turbines operate in saline spray zones with significant wave height above 2 m. Crack-prone blades with prior scarf repairs require quarterly borescope verification of adhesive bond lines.

### [w1-intervals:escalation]
If inspection logs show two consecutive findings of coating loss greater than 30 mm, planners accelerate maintenance intervals to 60-day drone inspections until erosion is remediated. Structural crack indications trigger immediate shutdown criteria if any growth beyond 5 mm is observed between sequential visits.

### [w1-intervals:documentation]
Maintenance supervisors must archive inspection imagery, NDT scan reports, and repair work orders in the computerized maintenance management system (CMMS) within 48 hours. This documentation supports warranty compliance and provides traceability for future risk assessments.
