### [w5-repair:field_guidance]
Field repairs for S2 erosion require surface sanding, moisture removal, and layered epoxy application. Proper curing below 70% relative humidity yields 18-month durability.

### [w5-repair:factory_threshold]
When erosion exposes structural laminate across more than 0.5 m of chord, factory repair is recommended to allow controlled curing and vacuum-assisted consolidation.

### [w5-repair:replacement_trigger]
Blade replacement is generally reserved for S4 cases where structural ribs are compromised or when combined damages make repair economics unfavorable relative to downtime costs.
