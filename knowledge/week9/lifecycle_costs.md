### [w9-costs:budget_impact]
A severity jump from S2 to S3 erosion increases levelized maintenance cost by approximately $18,000 per turbine due to longer downtime and specialized labor.

### [w9-costs:risk_weighting]
Applying risk-adjusted discount factors highlights that deferring repairs beyond 180 days yields negative net present value because of higher failure probabilities and lost production.

### [w9-costs:portfolio_planning]
Operators bundle repairs across turbines to capitalize on crew mobilization costs; combining three S2 cases into one campaign reduces per-unit expense by 25%.
