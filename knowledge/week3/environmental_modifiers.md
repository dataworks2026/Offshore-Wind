### [w3-environment:sea_spray]
Offshore turbines operating in high-salinity zones experience accelerated erosion because salt crystals increase abrasive loading. Studies indicate a 20% faster coating loss rate compared with inland sites under similar precipitation.

### [w3-environment:freeze_thaw]
Cold-weather regimes with frequent freeze-thaw cycles cause moisture ingress to expand micro-cracks. When combined with existing erosion, this mechanism can double the likelihood of laminate exposure within 180 days.

### [w3-environment:inspection_frequency]
Operators mitigate environmental accelerants by scheduling drone inspections every 60 days during winter months and applying hydrophobic coatings before peak storm seasons.
