### [w7-inspection:drone_vs_rope]
Drone inspections capture high-resolution imagery faster than rope access but require consistent lighting to identify micro-cracks. Rope access remains superior for tactile verification and small crack detection.

### [w7-inspection:ndt_roles]
Ultrasonic testing complements visual surveys by confirming subsurface delamination. Deploy NDT when visual cues suggest crack propagation beyond surface gelcoat.

### [w7-inspection:data_management]
Standardize photo naming conventions and embed GPS metadata to aid cross-season comparisons. Missing metadata reduces confidence in change-detection analytics.
