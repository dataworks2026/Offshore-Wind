### [w10-governance:change_control]
All updates to instructions, schemas, or knowledge require documented approval with version tags. This ensures traceability between GPT behavior and knowledge sources.

### [w10-governance:quality_review]
Quarterly audits compare GPT outputs against held-out cases to confirm calibration. Deviations trigger corrective action plans and retraining of few-shot examples.

### [w10-governance:data_privacy]
Inspection imagery must be anonymized before storage. Access controls should restrict personally identifiable information and comply with regional regulations.
