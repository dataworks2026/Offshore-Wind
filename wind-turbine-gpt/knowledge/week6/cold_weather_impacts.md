### [w6-icing:accumulation]
Ice accretion adds mass and alters aerodynamic loading, often masking erosion damage during winter inspections. Thermal imaging helps differentiate icing from substrate loss when visual cues are obscured.

### [w6-icing:deicing_schedule]
Deploying blade heating or chemical de-icing within 24 hours of ice detection reduces secondary erosion caused by uneven shedding. Missed treatments increase erosion progression probability by 0.15 over the next 90 days.

### [w6-icing:monitoring]
SCADA alarms for rotor imbalance combined with nacelle vibration exceedances above 1.2 mm/s RMS indicate icing severity that warrants immediate curtailment pending inspection.
